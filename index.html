You’re Not Alone.

I’ve been that business owner.

I’ve tried building businesses on my own. I’ve spent money I really didn’t have to waste on things I hoped would finally make the difference, without truly knowing whether I was fixing the right problem.

And I know how disheartening that can become.

Because eventually, the doubt starts creeping in:

  • Is my idea just not good enough?
  • Is my product the problem?
  • Do people even want what I’ve created?

And when you care deeply about what you’re building, those questions can start to feel personal.

That is one of the reasons I built Business & Brand Intelligence.

I wanted business owners to have a way to step back, look at the evidence, and understand what is actually happening before spending more money, more time, and more energy trying to fix the wrong thing.
